Fr. Davis Chiramel, known locally as Chiramelachan and more widely as the "Kidney Priest", is one of the best-known people to come from Aranattukara in recent times.

## From Valiyangadi to the priesthood

He was born at Aranattukara on 30 December 1960, the son of Chiramel Chakkunni and Kochannam, who lived at Valiyangadi. He went to [Tharakan's High School]({{ '/en/places/tharakans-school/' | relative_url }}), then to St. Mary's Minor Seminary in Thrissur and the Pontifical Seminary at Aluva. On his 28th birthday, 30 December 1988, Archbishop Mar Joseph Kundukulam ordained him a priest of the Thrissur diocese at [St. Thomas Church]({{ '/en/places/st-thomas-church/' | relative_url }}), Aranattukara.

## The Kidney Priest

The book says he first came to public attention when he gave one of his own kidneys to a poor man of another faith. His website dates the donation to 2009, at Lakeshore Hospital in Ernakulam. Out of that gift grew the **Kidney Federation of India**, a charitable trust based in Thrissur that he chairs. It helps people with end-stage kidney disease and encourages organ donation, and the book notes that it has led many kidney donations.

## Other work

He also founded **ACTS** (Accident Care and Transport Service), whose volunteers and ambulances take road-accident victims to hospital across Thrissur district. His [website](https://frdavischiramel.com/) lists many other projects, among them a cloth bank, food distribution, help for returning migrant workers during Covid, and housing for families in need. Alongside all this he continues his work as a priest and leads retreats. His website says he now serves under the Infant Jesus Church, Kadangode.
