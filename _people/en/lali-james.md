Lali James (also spelled Laly James) is one of the two councillors who represent Aranattukara in the Thrissur Municipal Corporation today. She was elected for the **Laloor** ward, which takes in St. Thomas Church and the market, in December 2025.

## In the council

She has been elected as a Congress (INC) candidate in four Corporation elections in a row:

- **2010:** Karyattukara
- **2015:** Laloor (then ward 50)
- **2020:** Karyattukara
- **2025:** Laloor (ward 47)

Newspapers in 2025 described her as a four-time councillor. You can see her alongside the other people who have represented Aranattukara on the [Councillors]({{ '/en/people/councillors/' | relative_url }}) page.

## The 2025 mayor election

After the Congress-led UDF won the Corporation in December 2025, Lali James was among those considered for mayor. The party chose Dr Niji Justin, who was elected mayor on 26 December 2025. Lali James voted for her.

The same day, Lali James publicly alleged that the mayor's post had been decided by money, and that the District Congress Committee president had asked her for funds. The DCC president, the KPCC president and Dr Justin all denied the allegations; the DCC president called them baseless. That night the KPCC suspended her from the party's primary membership. She said she stood by what she had said, and that she would remain a Congress supporter.
