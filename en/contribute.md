---
title: Contribute
summary: Share a memory, send an old photograph, suggest a correction, or list a business.
---

This site grows with what you share. Every contribution is read before it goes online, and we always credit you (or keep you anonymous, if you prefer).

- **A memory** — a few lines about a place, a person or a festival.
- **A photograph** — old or new, with when and where it was taken.
- **A correction** — tell us what is wrong and how you know.
- **A business** — name, address, phone and opening hours.
- **An event** — date, time, place and a poster if you have one.

*The contribution form opens in phase 2. Set `contribute_form` in `_config.yml` to a Google Form link, and every "Tell us" button will point to it.*
