## Stay connected

- **WhatsApp channel** — festival dates, news from home and new stories, once or twice a month.
- **Monthly newsletter** — the same, in your inbox, in English or Malayalam.

## Visiting home?

The [parish feast]({{ '/en/events/' | relative_url }}) is on the first Sunday of January. The [heritage walk]({{ '/en/map/' | relative_url }}) takes about an hour, and every stop has a QR code.

## Share your story

Where did life take you, and what do you remember of Aranattukara? [Tell us]({{ '/en/contribute/' | relative_url }}) — a few lines and a photo is enough.
