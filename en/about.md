aranattukara.org is a community website about Aranattukara, a neighbourhood of Thrissur city in Kerala ([at a glance]({{ '/en/at-a-glance/' | relative_url }})). It is written for the people who live here, for those who have moved away, and for the next generation.

## How we write history

We tell Aranattukara's history in our own words, from published sources, records and the memories of residents — and we say where each fact comes from. Where sources disagree, we say so. Corrections with evidence are always welcome.

## Photographs and privacy

We only publish photographs we own or have permission to use, and we credit them. We ask before writing about living people, and family pages are opt-in.

## Sources

Our most important printed source is Joy Chiramel's *Arranadu – Aranadu – Orunadu: Historical Faces of Aranattukara* (Esens Books, Thrissur, 2024). Buy a copy — it is a remarkable piece of local history.
