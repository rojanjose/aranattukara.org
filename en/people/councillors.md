Aranattukara has never had a council of its own. Until 2000 it was split between the **Thrissur Municipality** and the **Ayyanthole panchayat**. Since 2000 it has been part of the **Thrissur Corporation**, where the locality spans two wards: **Aranattukara** to the north, around the schools and convents, and **Laloor**, which takes in the old market and St. Thomas Church. See [Aranattukara at a glance]({{ '/en/at-a-glance/' | relative_url }}) for a map of both wards.

## Thrissur Corporation, since 2000

{% include councillors.html part="core" %}

The wards have been redrawn three times, so the numbers change: 52 wards in 2005, 55 from 2010 to 2020, and 56 from December 2025. In 2025 Aranattukara became ward 48 and Laloor ward 47.

The first Mayor of the Corporation, [Jose Kattukkaran]({{ '/en/people/jose-kattukkaran/' | relative_url }}), was from Aranattukara. The book says he was elected from the Elthuruth division in one place and Karyattukara in another; we have not yet found the 2000 results to settle it.

## Thrissur Municipality, before 2000

Thrissur got a town council in 1910 and an elected municipal council in 1921. The book remembers these councillors from Aranattukara. Few dates survive; the order is roughly the book's.
