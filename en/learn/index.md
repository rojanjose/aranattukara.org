## Glossary

| Word | Malayalam | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| *kara* | കര | Bank, shore — land that rises out of water |
| *kol* | കോൾ | Low-lying paddy fields that flood in the monsoon |
| *pandikasala* | പണ്ടികശാല | Warehouse for trade goods |
| *chunkam* | ചുങ്കം | Toll post; the toll collected there |
| *kadavu* | കടവ് | Landing place for boats |
| *tharavadu* | തറവാട് | Ancestral family house |
| *kavu* | കാവ് | Sacred grove |
| *perunnal* | പെരുന്നാൾ | Church feast |
