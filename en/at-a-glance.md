Aranattukara is a neighbourhood in the west of Thrissur city, about **2.5 km south-west of the Swaraj Round**, where the town meets the kol wetlands. It was once a village and a river port of its own; today it is part of the Thrissur Corporation.

## Boundaries

There is no single official boundary for "Aranattukara". The name covers a neighbourhood that today falls in two Corporation wards. The **Aranattukara ward** takes in the northern part, with the schools and convents. The old heart of the place, the market streets around **St. Thomas Church** and Tharakan's school, is in the **Laloor ward**.

{% include ward-map.html %}

Measured on the map, the Aranattukara ward covers about **1.9 km²** and the Laloor ward about **1.2 km²**. The old revenue village was larger: the book says it took in the *desams* (localities) of Aranattukara, Lalur, Karyattukara and Olari.

## Surrounding areas

Going round the two wards, starting in the north:

- **North:** Kanattukara and Ayyanthole, with the Civil Station to the north-west.
- **North-east:** Poothole, on the way to the Round.
- **South-east:** Vadookara.
- **South-west:** Karyattukara, which includes [St. Aloysius College]({{ '/en/places/st-aloysius-college/' | relative_url }}).
- **West:** Elthuruth, and beyond it the kol wetlands.

Laloor itself — the [Lalur temple]({{ '/en/places/lalur-karthyayani-temple/' | relative_url }}) and the old [trenching ground]({{ '/en/places/lalur-trenching-ground/' | relative_url }}) — lies within the Laloor ward, next door to Aranattukara's market.

## People

The census does not count Aranattukara on its own. It counts the whole **Thrissur Corporation** as one town, with **315,957 people in 2011**.

The 2011 census also published figures for each Corporation ward, using the ward numbers of the time. The ward that appears to be Aranattukara (ward 50 in the 2005 numbering) had:
