The Mathrubhumi Study Circle in Aranattukara was a small, independent group of young people with a love of books and writing.

## Meeting at the principal's house

Members used to gather at the home of Prof. O.J. Antony, principal of [St. Aloysius College]({{ '/en/places/st-aloysius-college/' | relative_url }}), an English scholar with a fine literary sense. The group's coordinator was Vincent Puthur, a writer and socialist activist. The book describes the members as bright, independent cultural workers, alert to new ideas.

## On stage

The circle is best remembered for its theatre. Its members staged *Parithranam*, written by Vincent Puthur and directed by John Kokkan, which won second place in a district drama competition at the Regional Theatre, Thrissur. They also performed *Agnihothram*, a musical drama about the Emergency, in the open auditorium of the Aranattukara church.

We do not yet know when the circle began or how long it lasted. If you were a member, please share your memories and photographs.
