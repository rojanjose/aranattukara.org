Near the [Government UP School]({{ '/en/places/gups-aranattukara/' | relative_url }}), close to the Eastern Bazaar, a board at a gate reads **Centre for Research and Development of Autistic Children – C.R.D.A.C.** and, below it, **Urban Resource Centre – URC Thrissur**. Above both is the emblem of Sarva Shiksha Abhiyan, the national programme for school education, with its slogan *vidyabhyasa avakasham*, "the right to education".

## What it is

Under Sarva Shiksha Abhiyan (run in Kerala today as Samagra Shiksha Kerala), each education sub-district has a resource centre, which supports schools, teachers and children with special needs. This building houses the one for urban Thrissur, together with a centre for autistic children.

It is one of the newer public institutions in Aranattukara, which has a long history of schools (see [Schools That Shaped Aranattukara]({{ '/en/stories/schools-that-shaped-aranattukara/' | relative_url }})).
