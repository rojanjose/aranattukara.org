The Karthyayani temple at Lalur is the oldest Hindu shrine of Aranattukara. It stands beside the road that leads from Lalur to the [Dr John Matthai Centre]({{ '/en/places/john-matthai-centre/' | relative_url }}) and on to the *kol* (low-lying paddy wetlands).

## A *kavu* temple

The book describes it as a *kavu* temple. A *kavu* is a sacred grove, and in early Kerala most village shrines were of this kind: an open place under great trees where the mother goddess of the village was worshipped. Many temples have since changed their form, but the word survives in their names, as it does in *Lalurkkavu*.

The presiding deity is Karthyayani, a gentle form of the Great Goddess (Adiparasakthi). Tradition counts the temple among the 108 Durga temples said to have been founded by Parasurama. It is also said that the Mannath family gave the land for it. The temple is under the Cochin Devaswom Board.

## Part of the Thrissur Pooram

Lalur is one of the eight temples whose goddesses and gods hold the *cherupoorams* (small poorams) that are part of the Thrissur Pooram. On Pooram day, morning and evening, the goddess sets out with three elephants to pay her respects to Vadakkunnathan.
