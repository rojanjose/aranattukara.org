St. Antony's Chapel stands on Poothole Road and is described in the book as a lively shrine of the saint. Locally it is also called the Poothole *kurishupalli* (chapel of the cross).

## A cemetery, then a shrine

The ground has its own history. In the years when Aranattukara had two rival churches, this was where the West Church buried its dead. The plot is generous, a little under three acres, which gives the chapel an open, spacious setting.

## Prayer here

The book records that Mass is said here every day. Around the saint's feast there is a litany, a daily novena and a votive feast.
