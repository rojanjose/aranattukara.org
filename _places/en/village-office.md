In Kerala, the land is divided into **revenue villages**, each with a village office of the state Revenue Department. Aranattukara is one of the revenue villages of Thrissur taluk. Its office today is called the **Aranattukara-Pullazhi Village Office**, a name that shows it also serves the neighbouring village of Pullazhi.

This is a state office, separate from the Thrissur Corporation, which runs the wards, roads and civic services. You can read how the two fit together in [Aranattukara at a glance]({{ '/en/at-a-glance/' | relative_url }}).

## What it does

The village office keeps the land records for the village, collects land tax, and issues or verifies certificates such as possession, income, residence and caste certificates, often applied for online and then checked by the village officer.

## Finding it

The office is on the Vatanappally–Kanjany–Thrissur road (State Highway 75) at Nedupuzha, Olarikkara, a little west of Aranattukara. It is outside the ward itself, because the revenue village is bigger than the ward.

- **Address:** Vatanappally–Kanjany–Thrissur Road, Nedupuzha, Olarikkara, Thrissur 680012
- **Phone:** [+91 85476 14420](tel:+918547614420)

Opening hours and phone numbers can change, so please confirm before you go.

## The old revenue village

The revenue village is older than the Corporation wards. In Cochin State days, Aranattukara village took in four *desams* (localities): Aranattukara, Lalur, Karyattukara and Olari. More about this in [What's in a Name]({{ '/en/stories/what-is-in-a-name/' | relative_url }}).
