The Holy Trinity Convent at Lalur is the Aranattukara branch of a congregation that began in Spain, the Trinitarian Sisters of Madrid. The book describes their life as one of prayer and good works in honour of the Holy Trinity.

## A short history

- **2008:** the house at Lalur is founded.
- **2010:** with the permission of Mar Andrews Thazhath, Archbishop of Thrissur, a house of religious formation opens here to train new sisters.
- **2011:** the sisters start the Holy Trinity Charitable Trust, which has since helped poor children with the cost of their schooling.
- **2018:** the parish opens the Angel Valley Pre-School in the [Tharakan's School]({{ '/en/places/tharakans-school/' | relative_url }}) compound, and the sisters have served there from the start.
- **2020:** a sister of this house becomes the first from India to take final vows in the congregation.

## Neighbours

The convent is close to the [Holy Trinity Ashram]({{ '/en/places/holy-trinity-ashram/' | relative_url }}) of the Trinitarian priests.
