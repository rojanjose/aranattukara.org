The Tagore Centenary Hall stands on Netaji Road, right beside the [Netaji Ground]({{ '/en/places/netaji-ground/' | relative_url }}), between Anchangadi Road and Poothole. Its name suggests that it was built to mark the birth centenary of the poet Rabindranath Tagore in 1961, as public halls in several Kerala towns were.

## A hall being rebuilt

In 2026 the site was a building site. Google Street View shows a new hall of three storeys going up, faced in laterite red with a tiled roof, with a tower crane and scaffolding in front. Google Maps marks the hall as "may be closed".

A Kerala Kaumudi report in November 2026, about a stalled Corporation hall at Ayyanthole, compared it with "the Netaji Hall at Aranattukara", suggesting that a hall here had also been held up. We think this may be the same building, but we have not been able to confirm it.
