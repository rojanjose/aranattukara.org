Pavanatma Convent stands on Convent Road in Aranattukara, a little south of the [Infant Jesus Convent]({{ '/en/places/infant-jesus-convent/' | relative_url }}). Like its older neighbour, it belongs to the Holy Family sisters. *Pavanatma* means the Holy Spirit.

## A ministry to families

The convent was founded on 23 December 1983. Its sisters chose a quiet kind of work: visiting Christian families at home and helping them with their troubles.

The book records that from 1991, for about five or six years, the community set its other work aside and lived as a house of prayer. Afterwards the sisters took up family ministry again, this time working together with the parish to strengthen family life.
