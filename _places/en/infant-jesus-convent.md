The Infant Jesus Convent belongs to the Congregation of the Holy Family. It was set up with one clear purpose: to give the girls of Aranattukara a high school of their own.

## A rented house at Padinjare Angadi

When Tharakan's School was raised to a high school in 1947–48, its girl pupils were handed over to the Holy Family sisters. The convent began on 26 May 1947 in a rented house belonging to Chiriyankandath Antony Michael at Padinjare Angadi, and a sister arrived to teach the girls' eighth class.

## A home of its own

The sisters later bought a coconut grove on the western side of Padinjare Angadi from Kuruthukulangara Koola Mathew, father of the well-known Varu Master. The book records that a parishioner, Chungath Rappai Joseph, gave generous help both with buying the land and with building a chapel for the convent.

In 1950 the classes became a separate school, the [Infant Jesus Girls' High School]({{ '/en/places/infant-jesus-girls-high-school/' | relative_url }}), which still stands beside the convent. In 1983 the same congregation opened a second house nearby, [Pavanatma Convent]({{ '/en/places/pavanatma-convent/' | relative_url }}).
