The Ayyappa temple stands in Kizhakkeppuram, the eastern side of Aranattukara. Its deity is Sastha, the name by which Ayyappan is worshipped in many Kerala temples.

## A young temple in an old neighbourhood

The book records that the temple was built in 1987 by members of the Parayil family. That makes it one of the newest shrines in the area, but the neighbourhood is old. The book notes that in earlier centuries Hindu families lived together in Kizhakkeppuram, in Lalur to the west and in Pattalangadi to the north, while the Christian traders clustered around the markets. It also stresses how closely the communities worked together, with Hindu neighbours joining in the church feasts and Hindu children studying in the parish schools.

The worship of Ayyappan, like that of the village mother goddess, goes back to the *kavu* (sacred grove) tradition that the book traces through all the older temples of the area.
