St. Sebastian's Chapel stands at Thekkekkara, also known as Vadookkara, across the water from old Aranattukara.

## A West Church chapel

When the parish was split between the East Church and the West Church, this *kurishupalli* (chapel of the cross) belonged to the West Church. The book recalls that there was Mass here every Sunday at six in the morning in those days.

Getting there was part of the story. Before the bridge over the boat canal, people had to cross by boat, and even after stepping off they still had a short walk to the chapel. It is a reminder of how much daily life in Aranattukara once depended on the water.

Today a road links Aranattukara and Vadookkara, and the boat journey is a memory.
