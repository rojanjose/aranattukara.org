St. Joseph's Chapel stands in Padinjare Angadi, the western market of Aranattukara. It is dedicated to St. Joseph.

## Five men named Ouseph

The book has only a short note about it, but it is a lovely one. In the early days, it is said, the chapel was built by five men of the market who were all called Ouseph, the Malayalam form of Joseph. Building a chapel to the saint they were named after seems a natural thing for them to have done.

Padinjare Angadi was one of the trading streets that made Aranattukara busy in the days of the port. A small chapel here, where traders and passers-by could stop and pray, fits the pattern of *kappelas* (wayside chapels) found all over Kerala.
