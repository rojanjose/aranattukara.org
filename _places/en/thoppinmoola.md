Thoppinmoola is a junction and bus stop on Anchangadi Road, where the road towards Poothole and Netaji Road branch off. Its name means "the corner of the grove" (*thoppu*, a grove, and *moola*, a corner). In the evenings it fills up with autorickshaws, scooters and shoppers.

## A corner for clubs

Two of Aranattukara's best-known clubs grew up here.

- The **Athaneeyam Club**, started in 1942, met in the Thoppinmoola house of **A.C. Lona Master**. He was the headmaster of the church school until 1928, when he was dismissed for publishing a book arguing that there is no God, and he died of tuberculosis four years later. His house became the home of Aranattukara's first organised drama club, whose play *Meridasan* is still remembered.
- The **Netaji Sports Club** worked out of a shed belonging to Vaimel Mani at Thoppinmoola, and ran the football tournaments on the nearby [Netaji Ground]({{ '/en/places/netaji-ground/' | relative_url }}).

You can read more about both in [Arts, Drama and Clubs]({{ '/en/stories/arts-drama-and-clubs/' | relative_url }}).

## Old houses

Among the tiled houses that trading families built in the nineteenth and early twentieth centuries, the book names two that rose to three storeys. One was the Chungath house at Valiyangadi. The other was the S.M.S. house at Thoppinmoola.

## Thoppinmoola today

Thoppinmoola is one of the busiest commercial corners in Aranattukara, with salons, shops, clinics and offices around the junction. Find them in the [Business Directory]({{ '/en/business/' | relative_url }}).
