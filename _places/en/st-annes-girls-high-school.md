St. Ann's Girls' High School serves the northern side of Aranattukara.

## What we know

The book has only a short note about St. Ann's. It was founded by the CMC sisters, the Congregation of the Mother of Carmel, to give the girls of northern Aranattukara a school close to home. It later grew into a high school. The sisters now also run a self-financing women's college, [Nirmala College]({{ '/en/places/nirmala-college/' | relative_url }}), a few minutes' walk west at the Chungam junction.

The CMC is one of the congregations that St. Kuriakose Elias Chavara helped to found, the same saint who founded the [Elthuruth Ashram]({{ '/en/places/elthuruth-ashram/' | relative_url }}) on the western edge of the parish. So in a sense the two ends of Aranattukara owe schools to the same man's vision of "a school for every church".
