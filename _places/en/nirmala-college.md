Nirmala College stands at the Chungam junction on Anchangadi Road, which takes its name from the old Kanjani road toll post, the [*chunkam*]({{ '/en/places/toll-gate/' | relative_url }}). The bus stop there is still called "Nirmala College". It is in the Aranattukara ward of the Thrissur Corporation, a few minutes' walk west of [St. Anne's Girls' High School]({{ '/en/places/st-annes-girls-high-school/' | relative_url }}).

## What we know

The book mentions the college in one line, in its note on St. Anne's. The CMC sisters (the Congregation of the Mother of Carmel) founded St. Anne's for the girls of northern Aranattukara, and a self-financing women's college, Nirmala College, now works alongside it. The Nirmalalayam nursery school stands next to the college.

The CMC is one of the congregations that [St. Kuriakose Elias Chavara]({{ '/en/people/st-kuriakose-elias-chavara/' | relative_url }}) helped to found.
